I also like watching the news to stay informed about what is happening in the world. Knowing about current events helps me understand different points of view.

Formative Component Entry #5

Critical Thinking and Fake News

Today, we receive a lot of information on social media and the internet every minute. Some news is true, but some information is false (fake news). Critical thinking means checking the information before believing or sharing it.

I always ask: Who wrote this? Is this source reliable? It is very important to be a responsible internet user and share only real information.

Formative Component Entry #6

Reflection on Crime and Punishment

Crime and punishment are important topics for every society. Laws exist to keep us safe and maintain order. In my opinion, justice is not only about punishing people who break the law, but also about helping them change and learn.

Education and job opportunities can help people make better choices and build a safer community for everyone.
