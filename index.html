Family & Personal Life Entry #1

Welcome to My Blog & My World!

Hi everyone! My name is Gabriela. Currently, I live in Itagüí with my partner, and we enjoy our cozy life here. However, my family lives in Caucasia, Antioquia. My family is composed of my parents, my three sisters, and my little niece. Even though we live in different cities, we talk all the time and stay very close.

I love visiting them when I have free time because spending time with my family always gives me energy and peace!

Hobbies & Leisure Entry #2

My Favorite Hobby: Reading

In my free time, one of my favorite activities is reading. Books allow me to travel to different worlds and learn new ideas. Right now, I am reading the classic book The Strange Case of Dr. Jekyll and Mr. Hyde by Robert Louis Stevenson. It is a very interesting mystery story about human nature!

Besides reading books, I also enjoy studying English every day to improve my vocabulary and practice my pronunciation.

Work & Professional Growth Entry #3

My Busy Daily Routine: Work and Study

My daily schedule is very active! I wake up early at 5:30 a.m., take a quick shower, and have breakfast. At 7:30 a.m., I start my work day at Fundación Grupo EPM, where I work on social and community projects.

After work at 5:00 p.m., I dedicate my evening to my academic goals. I study at SENA and also at Universidad de Antioquia. Managing work and two educational programs requires discipline, but I love learning and growing professionally every single day. I usually go to sleep around 10:30 p.m.

Travel & Current Events Entry #4

Traveling Between Itagüí and Caucasia

Because my family lives in Caucasia and I live in Itagüí, traveling is an important part of my life. I love watching the green landscapes of Antioquia during the bus trips. Traveling helps me disconnect from stress and appreciate nature.

I also like reading global news online to stay informed about environmental issues and social topics around the world.

Formative Component Entry #5

Critical Thinking and Media Literacy

Every day we receive hundreds of messages on Instagram, WhatsApp, and news portals. Critical thinking means we should not believe everything we see immediately. We must check the sources of the news and verify if the information is true.

As a student at Universidad de Antioquia and SENA, media literacy helps me evaluate data carefully and share only truthful information with my community.

Formative Component Entry #6

Reflections on Crime and Punishment

Crime and punishment are essential discussions for modern society. Through my work at Fundación Grupo EPM, I believe that social development and education are the best tools to prevent crime.

Instead of only punishing people, society should offer education, jobs, and social opportunities. This helps people make better decisions and creates a safer environment for everyone.
